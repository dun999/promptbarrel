Fill a tank

 
Barrels you can fill – Connect a wallet

The tank gets its own spend key, made in this browser. Give it to an agent: it can pay for proven answers and nothing else. With an agent account it is a job tank: the agent thinks on this money, and when you accept the work, whatever is left is paid to the agent in USDC. Reject it and the rest comes back to you. Filling takes a 0.01 NEAR storage deposit.

What a receipt does not prove

v1

Which machine served it

A TEE holding the model's key signed these bytes. The receipt does not identify the serving instance.

Who registered the key

Barrel's registrar checks attestations off‑chain. barrel audit repeats the checks.

GPU evidence

NVIDIA GPU evidence is not checked yet. Guarantees rest on Intel TDX.

Prompt privacy

The operator's Pump sees prompts and answers in plaintext.

Streaming

Streamed answers are signed by the gateway, so the Pump refuses to stream.

Real money, low caps

This is mainnet USDC. The contract caps how much it holds, and it is upgradeable by its owner key.
