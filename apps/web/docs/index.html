Documentation for Barrel v1.

Barrel holds prepaid AI inference in a NEAR contract and pays the operator per answer, only when the model's own TEE has signed it. This page covers using it, handing it to an agent, the protocol underneath, and running an operator.

01

Overview

Barrel sells prepaid inference from one named model. You pay USDC into a NEAR contract and receive Barrels. The operator who serves the model is paid out of that escrow per answer, and only after the contract has checked two signatures: the model's, made inside its TEE over the exact request and answer, and your client's acknowledgement of that answer. Whatever you do not spend comes back to you.

No proof, no ack. No ack, no payment.A cheaper model swapped in, or one changed byte in the answer, fails the check, and the units stay in your tank.
  1. 1Buy

    USDC becomes Barrels. The USDC waits in the contract's escrow.

  2. 2Fill

    Barrels go into a tank under a spend key that can pay for answers and nothing else.

  3. 3Ask

    The request goes to the operator's Pump and on to the model's TEE on NEAR AI Cloud.

  4. 4Settle

    Your client checks the model's signature and signs an ack. The contract checks both and pays.

  5. 5Refund

    After the series closes, unused Barrels burn for the USDC behind them.

Barrel runs on NEAR Protocol (the contract, the escrow and the native ed25519_verify), NEAR AI Cloud (models in Intel TDX and NVIDIA confidential computing, with per-answer signatures), and NEAR Intents (USDC from other chains).

02

Quick start

You need a NEAR mainnet account in MyNearWallet, HOT or Intear with a little NEAR for gas and the 0.01 NEAR tank deposit, and USDC, either on NEAR or on another chain.

  1. 01
    Connect

    Open the app and connect your wallet. Every write is a transaction your wallet shows you first.

  2. 02
    Buy Barrels

    Pick a series, enter an amount of USDC and sign. Or pay with USDC on another chain: the app gets you a deposit address from NEAR Intents and the Barrels land in your NEAR account.

  3. 03
    Fill a tank

    Your browser makes a fresh spend key and keeps it in local storage. Your wallet signs fill with the key's public half and a 0.01 NEAR storage deposit.

  4. 04
    Ask

    The browser signs the request with the tank key and sends it to the series' Pump. It checks the model's signature itself and with the contract's free verify_receipt view, and only then signs the ack. The app links the settlement transaction.

  5. 05
    Hand it to an agent

    The app shows a claude mcp add command with the tank id and key filled in, and offers the key as a file.

  6. 06
    Take the rest back

    Unlock a tank and withdraw its Barrels after 10 minutes. After the series closes, refund unused Barrels for their USDC.

Before you buy

Asking needs the series' Pump at an address your browser can reach: a public HTTPS address, or this machine. The app marks a series whose Pump it cannot reach before you buy, and Deployments below shows where each series points right now.

03

Concepts

Barrel
1,000,000 units of inference from one series. A NEP-245 token whose id is the series id, with 6 decimals, so one Barrel reads as 1.0.
Unit
What an answer is metered in: one completion token, plus a weighted share of each prompt token.
Grade
A model id and its prompt-token weight, for example glm-5.3-flash → z-ai/glm-5.3-flash at 16%. Model signing keys are registered per grade.
Series
One operator selling one grade at a fixed price until a closing time, with a cap on units and the address of its Pump. Anyone can open a series.
Escrow
The USDC behind minted Barrels. It leaves only as operator pay for a proven answer, as a refund, or as a job tank's payout to its agent.
Tank
Barrels set aside under a spend key. The account that filled it owns it.
Spend key
An Ed25519 key made for one tank. It signs requests and acks. It cannot transfer, withdraw or refund.
Job tank
A tank filled for another account, the agent. If the owner accepts the work, the leftover is paid to the agent in USDC.
Pump
The operator's gateway. It serves funded tanks, relays the exact bytes to NEAR AI Cloud and back, and settles acks on chain.
Receipt
The model id, the hashes of the request and the answer, and the model's signing key and signature over them.
Ack
The spend key's signature over the contract, tank, sequence number, both hashes and the units billed.
Use
04

Buying Barrels

With USDC on NEAR

A buy is one USDC transfer to the contract, ft_transfer_call with the message {"series_id": 1}. The contract mints amount × 1,000,000 ÷ price units to you, or to the account named in "to". At 0.60 USDC per Barrel, 1 USDC buys 1.666666 Barrels. Any part of the amount that would go past the series cap comes back in the same transaction, and a buy past the contract's total escrow cap is refused and returned.

With USDC on another chain

The app and the CLI quote a NEAR Intents 1Click swap whose recipient is the Barrel contract and whose message is {"series_id": N, "to": "<your NEAR account>"}. You send one deposit on the origin chain; 1Click swaps it to USDC on NEAR and delivers it with ft_transfer_call, and the contract mints the Barrels to your account. You never hold USDC on NEAR.

  • An EVM wallet in the browser can send the deposit for you; on any other chain, send the quoted amount to the deposit address yourself.
  • A swap not finished by the quote's deadline is refunded to your address on the origin chain.
  • Quotes are kept under the series' room and the global escrow cap, because a delivery the contract refuses goes back to NEAR Intents rather than to you.
  • Without a 1Click JWT (ONECLICK_JWT, CLI only) 1Click adds a 0.25% fee.
pnpm barrel buy-from base 1 1.0 --refund-to 0xYourBaseAddress --account you.near
05

Tanks and spend keys

fill(series_id, units, key, agent?) moves Barrels from your balance into a new tank under the public half of a spend key, with a 0.01 NEAR storage deposit. In the app the key is made in your browser and kept in its local storage for this site, per contract; you can download it as a file, and import a key from a file or from .barrel/tanks/. The CLI writes keys to .barrel/tanks/<contract>/<tank>.json.

Answers settle strictly in order: a tank has a next sequence number, and every request and ack names it. top_up(tank_id, units) adds Barrels and cancels an unlock in progress.

StateMeaning
ActiveThe Pump serves it. Its key can pay for proven answers.
UnlockingThe owner asked for the rest back. The Pump no longer serves it. Withdraw after the unlock delay, or top up to keep using it.
ClosedWithdrawn, or a job tank whose leftover went to the agent or back to the owner.
06

Asking

The app, the CLI and the MCP server all ask the same way (TankClient in packages/sdk, and apps/web/src/tank.ts in the browser):

  1. Build a non-streamed chat request for the series' model with a max_tokens.
  2. Work out the most units the request could cost, sign the request text with the tank key, and send it to the series' Pump.
  3. Receive the model's exact answer bytes with a receipt and the units billed.
  4. Check that the signature is the model TEE's (provider_tee), that the signed text names the model and both hashes, that the hashes match the bytes sent and received, that the contract's verify_receipt finds the signature valid and the key registered and live, and that the units match the answer's usage.
  5. Only then sign the ack and send it. The Pump settles it on chain.

If any check fails, no ack is signed and nothing is paid. The Pump serves a tank one answer at a time and will not serve it again until the last answer is acknowledged, so an operator can lose at most one unpaid answer per tank.

07

Job tanks

Fund an agent and pay it what it does not spend. A client fills a tank naming another account as its agent and gives the agent the spend key. The agent pays for its own proven inference out of the tank while it works. Every answer it paid for is on chain with the model's signature, so the client sees what the job cost before deciding.

CallWhoEffect
fill(series, units, key, agent)ClientFunds the job. The agent gets the spend key.
ask … settleAgent, PumpEach answer is paid on proof.
accept(tank)ClientAccepts the work. The Pump stops serving the tank.
withdraw(tank)Client or agentAfter the unlock delay, pays the leftover's USDC to the agent.
request_unlock(tank)ClientRejects the work. After the unlock delay the leftover comes back to the client.

The agent's account must be registered with the USDC token to receive it. If it is not, the payout bounces, the tank is restored and nothing is lost; the app offers to open the agent's USDC account (storage_deposit) and pay again.

08

Withdrawals and refunds

  • Unlock a tank. request_unlock(tank), then withdraw(tank) after the unlock delay (10 minutes) returns its Barrels to your balance. The delay lets answers already served settle first.
  • Refund Barrels. After the series closes and the settle grace (1 hour) has passed, refund(series, units) burns Barrels for floor(units × price ÷ 1,000,000) USDC. Series 1 closes Dec 31, 2026, 00:00 UTC.
  • Move Barrels. Barrels in your account are NEP-245 tokens: mt_transfer and mt_transfer_call work as the standard says.

MCP server

apps/mcp is an MCP server over stdio that gives an agent one tank. The agent holds only the tank's spend key, so the most it can do is spend the tank on answers that carry a valid model signature. With a tank filled in the app, use the command it shows:

  -e BARREL_NETWORK=mainnet \
  -e BARREL_CONTRACT=barrel-v1.promptbarrel.near \
  -e BARREL_TANK=<tank id> \
  -e BARREL_TANK_KEY=<spend key> \
  -- node /path/to/barrel/apps/mcp/bin/barrel-mcp.mjs

11

HTTP 402 and x402

Not in v1

Barrel v1 does not implement x402. There is no x402 payment-requirements response, no X-PAYMENT header and no facilitator. Inference is paid from a tank, settled on NEAR per answer against the model's signature.

What the Pump does use is the HTTP status 402 Payment Required in its plain sense: it answers 402, with a JSON error, when a tank cannot pay for a request.

StatusReason the Pump gives
402The tank is closed.
402The client accepted this job; the tank is paying out its leftover.
402The tank is unlocking; top it up to keep using it.
402The series has expired.
402Acknowledge the previous answer first.
402The tank holds fewer units than this request may need.

Why not x402 per request: an x402 payment settles before the answer exists, while Barrel pays only after the model's signature over the finished answer checks out. On the roadmap, x402 comes in as a way to fund a tank, not to pay per answer: a Pump would answer a request that comes with no tank with 402 and an x402 offer, and the payment would buy Barrels into a tank for the payer's key. That is not built.

Protocol
12

Pricing and units

units = completion_tokens + ⌈prompt_tokens × input_weight_bps ÷ 10,000⌉

Series 1 sells z-ai/glm-5.3-flash at 0.60 USDC per Barrel with prompt tokens weighted 16%. That is 0.60 USDC per million completion tokens and 0.096 USDC per million prompt tokens. An answer with 420 prompt and 180 completion tokens costs 180 + 68 = 248 units, about 0.00015 USDC.

  • Before serving, the Pump requires a ceiling: the request's bytes counted as prompt tokens, plus max_tokens. The request signature commits to it, the tank must hold it, and the bill is the metered units, never more than the ceiling.
  • The operator is paid floor(cumulative_burned × price ÷ 1,000,000) minus what was already released, so rounding never adds up against either side.
13

Signatures

Three Ed25519 signatures, over three texts. Hashes are SHA-256 of the exact bytes, as 64 lowercase hex characters.

Model, inside its TEE

<model_id>:<sha256(request)>:<sha256(response)>

Request, by the tank key

barrel:v1:req:<contract>:<tank>:<seq>:<sha256(request)>:<max_units>

Ack, by the tank key

barrel:v1:ack:<contract>:<tank>:<seq>:<request_hash>:<response_hash>:<units>

NEAR AI Cloud signs every non-streamed answer with the model's key. Streamed answers are signed by the gateway instead, over text that names no model, so the Pump refuses streaming and the contract would never pay a gateway signature: the gateway's key is not registered for any grade. A receipt looks like this:

{
  "grade_id": "glm-5.3-flash",
  "model_id": "z-ai/glm-5.3-flash",
  "request_hash": "29809c43…2437",
  "response_hash": "20c229a6…3525",
  "signing_algo": "ed25519",
  "signing_address": "5a0210f2…caff",
  "signature": "49603d99…",
  "signature_kind": "provider_tee",
  "text": "z-ai/glm-5.3-flash:29809c43…:20c229a6…",
  "request_b64": "…",
  "response_b64": "…"
}

Check one in the browser on the app's Verify a receipt page, or with pnpm barrel verify <file>.

14

Settlement

settle(tank_id, items) takes 1 to 40 items. Each carries seq, request_hash, response_hash, units, ack_sig, tee_key and tee_sig. For each one, before any money moves, the contract checks:

  1. The sequence number is the tank's next one: no replay, no reordering.
  2. The ack verifies under the tank's spend key.
  3. The model key is registered for the series' grade and has not expired or been revoked.
  4. The model signature verifies over <model_id>:<request_hash>:<response_hash>, with model_id taken from the grade, not from the caller.
  5. The units fit in the tank.

Then it burns the units and pays the series operator. Signatures use NEAR's native ed25519_verify: the first item costs about 8.6 TGas including the payout, each further item about 1 TGas. Settlement is accepted until the series closes plus the settle grace. A payout the token refuses is kept as payable, and the operator collects it with claim(series_id).

15

Attestation

The contract pays only signatures from keys registered for the grade. Before the owner registers a key with add_key, Barrel's registrar checks NEAR AI Cloud's attestation for the model:

  • Intel DCAP verification of the TDX quote, with Intel's collateral.
  • The report data binds the signing key and a fresh nonce.
  • The event log replays to RTMR3, which shows what software holds the key.

The evidence (report, nonce, collateral) is published in deployments/evidence/, and its hash is stored with the key on chain. Keys expire after the grade's time to live (24 hours for series 1). When the key is about to lapse, a Pump that holds the owner key attests again and registers the new key; without it, answers whose key is not live are refused and not billed. Anyone can repeat the checks:

pnpm barrel attest z-ai/glm-5.3-flash    # → ATTESTED
pnpm barrel audit                        # registered keys vs. a fresh attestation

NVIDIA GPU evidence is not checked yet. The checks follow NEAR AI's reference verifier, nearai-cloud-verifier.

Pump API

A Pump serves one series. Clients normally use TankClient; this is the wire format underneath.

RouteBodyReturns
GET /, GET /v1/info–Contract, series, operator, model, price, input weight, closing time, unlock delay, answers served, units settled.
POST /v1/chat/completionsOpenAI-style chat request for the series' model. max_tokens 1 to 4096, no stream.The model's exact answer bytes, with headers x-barrel-receipt (base64 receipt JSON), x-barrel-units and x-barrel-seq.
POST /v1/ack{"tank_id", "seq", "ack_sig"}{"ok", "queued", "settle_tx"}

A completion request carries four headers: x-barrel-tank, x-barrel-seq, x-barrel-max-units, and x-barrel-sig, the tank key's signature over the request text in Signatures.

StatusWhen
400Body not JSON, another model, streaming asked for, max_tokens out of range, or a ceiling below the request's worst case.
401Missing headers, or a request not signed by the tank key.
402The tank cannot pay. See HTTP 402 and x402.
404The tank is not on this series, or no answer is waiting for this ack.
409A request for this tank is already in flight, or the sequence number is not the next one.
413The body is over 1 MB.
502The model returned no signature, an unusable one, one the contract would not accept, or no usage. That answer is not billed.

Run a Pump

The operator runs one Pump per series. It needs the operator account's key in .barrel/mainnet/ and a NEAR AI Cloud API key as NEARAI_API_KEY in .env.

# serve it behind TLS at a public https:// address
PUMP_HOST=0.0.0.0 PUMP_PORT=8787 pnpm pump

A browser reaches a Pump only over HTTPS, or on its own machine, so the series must point at a public HTTPS address before anyone else can ask. The operator (op-v1.promptbarrel.near) moves it by calling set_series_endpoint on the contract, Barrel.setSeriesEndpoint in packages/sdk. The endpoint only says where to ask; the payment rules do not change with it.

VariableDefaultMeaning
BARREL_NETWORKmainnetWhich deployment record to serve.
PUMP_HOST, PUMP_PORT127.0.0.1, 8787Where to listen.
PUMP_SERIESthe last seriesWhich series to serve.
PUMP_OPERATORthe recorded operatorWhich account settles and gets paid.
PUMP_SETTLE_MS00 settles each ack at once; otherwise acks are batched on this interval.

Acknowledged answers not yet settled are kept in a journal under .barrel/pump/ and flushed to the chain on SIGINT or SIGTERM. When the machine also holds the owner key, the Pump re-attests and re-registers the model key as it rotates.

Trust and limits

What a receipt proves: a TEE holding a key registered for this model signed these exact request and answer bytes. What it does not:

Which machine served it

The receipt does not identify the serving instance.

Who registered the key

Barrel's registrar checks attestations off chain. The evidence is published and barrel audit repeats the checks. Moving them on chain is stage 3.

GPU evidence

NVIDIA GPU evidence is not checked yet. Guarantees rest on Intel TDX and NVIDIA confidential-computing assumptions.

Prompt privacy

The operator's Pump sees prompts and answers in plaintext.

Availability

An operator can refuse to serve. The money stays safe, but its refund waits for the series to close. An operator can lose at most one unpaid answer per tank.

Real money, low caps

Mainnet escrow is capped at 50 USDC in total while the contract is new, and the owner key can upgrade the contract.
