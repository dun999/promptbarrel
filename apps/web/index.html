Frequently asked questions

What is Barrel?

Prepaid AI inference with proof of which model ran. You pay USDC into a NEAR contract. The operator is paid per answer, and only after the contract checks that the named model's TEE signed that exact answer. What you don't use comes back to you.

What does an answer cost?

You buy Barrels of a model at its series' fixed price: 0.60 USDC per Barrel for z-ai/glm-5.3-flash. One Barrel is 1,000,000 units. An answer costs one unit per completion token plus 16% of a unit per prompt token.

When does the operator get paid?

Per answer, when it settles. Before any money moves, the contract checks that the answer is the tank's next one, that your agent signed an ack for it, that the model key is registered and live, that the model's signature covers this exact request and answer, and that the units fit in the tank. Then it pays the operator for those units.

What does the model's signature prove?

That a TEE holding the model's registered key signed the model name and the hashes of this request and this answer. A cheaper model swapped in, or one changed byte in the answer, fails the check and nothing is paid. The key is registered only after an Intel TDX attestation check, and the evidence is published.

What does a receipt not prove?

It does not identify the machine that served the answer. NVIDIA GPU evidence is not checked yet. In this version Barrel's registrar checks attestations off-chain, though anyone can repeat the checks with barrel audit. The operator sees prompts and answers in plaintext.

What happens if a check fails?

Your agent does not sign the ack, so the operator cannot settle and nothing is paid. The units stay in your tank.

How do I give my agent a tank?

In the app, buy Barrels and fill a tank. The tank gets its own spend key, and the app gives you a claude mcp add command with it filled in. The key can pay for proven answers and nothing else: it cannot transfer, withdraw or refund.

How do I get unused money back?

Unlock a tank, wait 10 minutes, and withdraw its Barrels to your account. When the series closes, refund the Barrels you hold for the USDC behind them.

Do I need real money?

Yes. Barrel runs on NEAR mainnet with Circle's USDC. Pay with USDC on NEAR from a NEAR wallet, or with USDC on another chain through NEAR Intents. While the contract is new it caps how much escrow it holds in total.

A tank, not a wallet.

Connect a NEAR wallet, buy Barrels, fill a tank, and hand your agent its spend key over MCP. It pays only for answers the model can prove.
